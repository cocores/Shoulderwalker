Shoulder Ride

Walk as far as you can with the little one on your shoulders.

Hold a leglift it and swing it forward. Let goit plants where it meets the ground. Walkalternate: hold, let go, hold the other. Both feet down = stand still. Leanforward keeps your momentum, back slows you. The baby exaggerates every wobble.

Keys: Q lean back, W left leg, O right leg, P lean forward
